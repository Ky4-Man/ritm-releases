5. Payment

Transfer in rubles using the details in the app (Pro screen), then attach a screenshot of the transfer to the request. Pro opens after the payment is checked, usually within a day. The screenshot is deleted from the cloud once the request has been reviewed; it stays in the seller’s private Telegram chat.

Access can also be granted with an activation code: a code works once and opens Pro for the stated period.

6. No auto-renewal

Each period is paid separately; the app reminds you 3 days before the end. A new payment is added to the time you have left.

7. When the period ends

All your records stay and basic tracking works as usual. Pro features close until the next payment.

8. Refunds

Within 14 days of payment: a full refund on request to the email above, no reason needed. Later: for unused full months of a yearly period. Money is returned the same way within 10 days.

9. App store purchases

If you subscribed through Google Play, payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled by Google Play under its own rules.

10. Outages

If Pro does not work through our fault for more than 3 days in a row, the paid period is extended by that time.
