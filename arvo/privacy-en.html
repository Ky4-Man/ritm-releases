Arvo

Privacy policy

Effective from October 24, 2026.

About this document

This document explains what data the Arvo app uses, where it is stored and how to delete it. Arvo is an app for tracking spending, income, savings and debts.

What is stored on your phone

Everything you enter: spending and income, accounts and balances, categories, limits, goals, debts, projects, tags, notes and settings. This data is stored only on your phone, in the app database. The developer does not see or receive it. The app lock code is stored only as a hash; fingerprint unlock is checked by Android itself — the app never receives your fingerprint. If you turn on drafts from bank notifications, the app reads notifications only from the bank apps you chose, and only on the phone: notification text is never sent anywhere. The app does not read SMS.

Exchange rates

To convert amounts between currencies, the app downloads exchange rates from open.er-api.com and api.coingecko.com a few times a day. The request contains none of your data — only a list of currencies. As with any internet request, these services see your connection address (IP).

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app saves a backup file once a day to the hidden app folder in your Drive. The app can access only that folder; your other Drive files are not visible to it. The backup is kept in your Google account under Google terms.

Cloud and family (optional)

If you sign in with Google under “Account and cloud”, your records are also stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, servers in the USA) so you can restore them on a new phone and share them with your family. Sign-in uses your Google name, email and profile photo — family members can see them. Family members see only the shared accounts, goals and records you made shared; any record can be hidden from the family. Only you and your family can access this data; the developer uses it for nothing but running the app.

What the app does not do

No ads and no advertising ID. No third-party analytics or tracking. Data is not sold or shared with third parties. The app does not read SMS, contacts or location. Notifications (reminders, evening summary) are created on the phone itself.

How to delete your data

On the phone: uninstall the app — all records are deleted with it (the auto backup in “Downloads/Arvo”, if you turned it on, stays — delete the file manually). In the cloud: “More → Account and cloud → Delete account and cloud data” — records are removed from the cloud immediately. Google Drive backup: “More → Disconnect Google Drive”, then in Google Drive open “Settings → Manage apps” and delete the Arvo data. If something does not work, contact the developer and we will delete the data within 30 days.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the website with a new date. Questions about your data can be sent to the developer via “More → About → Contact the developer”.

Contact: arvo.app.help@gmail.com
