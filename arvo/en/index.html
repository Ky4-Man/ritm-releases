FAQ

Is Arvo free?

Yes, the core is free and ad-free: expenses and income, limits, “safe to spend today”, savings goals, debts, currencies, weekly and monthly reports, export. The Pro subscription adds cloud sync and family sharing, the AI advisor, unlimited receipt scans and Net worth.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. No sign-up needed. Cloud sync and Google Drive backup are used only if you turn them on. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does Arvo read SMS or connect to my bank?

No. Arvo never asks for SMS access, bank passwords or card details. You log expenses yourself — it takes two taps.

Can I track several currencies?

Yes. Each account has its own currency; totals are shown in your main currency at the rate of the day. Crypto is supported too: USDT, BTC, ETH, TON.

How do I move from another app?

Arvo imports records from a CSV file, which almost every budgeting app can export.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Android only (Android 8 and newer).

Which languages?

English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese and Vietnamese — Arvo follows your phone language.
