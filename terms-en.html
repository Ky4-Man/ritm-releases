2. Your data

Your schedule, tasks and notes are stored on your phone. How the app handles data is described in the privacy policy. Keep backups: the app saves an automatic copy once a day to “Downloads/Ritm”, but data may be lost if the phone breaks or is lost.

We do everything we can to make reminders and alarms fire on time. But the phone itself can limit them: battery saving modes, manufacturer settings, disabled permissions. Don’t rely on Ritm as the only means where missing a reminder is dangerous: medication, important flights, shifts.
