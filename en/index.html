Can’t do it? Tap “Can’t”

Ritm suggests what to swap or where to move it without breaking the rest of your day. Replace, extend, drag and swipe — the plan changes in a couple of taps.

Your day and week

Answer three questions on first launch and your week is ready: wake-up, work, sport, rest, sleep. Week templates are easy to tweak.

Tasks and notes

Quick input in plain words: “call tomorrow at 3 pm”. Due dates, repeats, folders, tags, checklists and voice notes. Import from TickTick and Todoist.

Alarms that actually ring

Up to five reminders per activity: a notification or a real alarm, even on a locked screen. Home screen widgets and a calm night theme.

Ritm Family

Shared tasks (“buy milk → Anna”), the family day with the time everyone is free, kids’ schedules and shared shopping lists. Everyone chooses what of their day to show. 14 days free.

FAQ

Is Ritm free?

Yes. Everything except Family is free, with no ads. Family is a paid subscription for the whole family, with the first 14 days free.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. A cloud copy is made only if you sign in with Google yourself. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Android only (Android 7 and newer).

Which languages?

English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
